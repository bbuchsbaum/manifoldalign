Interpretation
--------------
At 220,000 total nodes, exact bounded kNN relation construction accounts for
57.74% of sampled time, almost entirely inside RANN::nn2. Repeated sparse UOT
evaluation accounts for 26.39%. Together they account for 84.13% of sampled
time. Diffusion, prototype relation contraction, matrix conversion, and all
other sampled work are secondary.

The million task has 4.54546 times as many common nodes as the profile probe
but consumed more than 8.4503 times its profiled fit time. Treating the timeout
as right censoring gives a conservative runtime exponent lower bound of 1.4095
between these two configurations. Profiling overhead inflates the smaller-run
denominator, so it cannot explain away the superlinear lower bound.

Filing-ready optimization targets
---------------------------------
1. Replace monolithic exact RANN queries for feature, position, and attribute
   graphs with reusable approximate indexes and bounded query batches. Use a
   linear spatial-grid path for low-dimensional common-coordinate positions.
2. Record per-view index-build and query times in the fit result, including
   requested k, approximation tolerance, edges retained, and recall checks on
   a frozen smaller oracle.
3. Warm-start sparse UOT dual variables across alternating evaluations and
   avoid recomputing unchanged candidate costs. Preserve the current exact
   solver as the numerical oracle.
4. Add a performance profile that is selected before evaluation and verify its
   classification/matching deltas against the exact profile on untouched
   seeds before another million-node attempt.
5. Repeat the same 1,000,002-node gate under the unchanged 1,200-second and
   30-GB ceilings. Do not claim completion from extrapolation.
